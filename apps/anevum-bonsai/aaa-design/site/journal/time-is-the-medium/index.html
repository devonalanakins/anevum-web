Field Journal / 15 November 2026

Time is
the medium.

An inaugural editorial statement about a new collection, not a fabricated horticultural field report.

001
Editorial draft / 2026

ANEVUM begins with five living red maples, grown from seed in 2026. They do not look like the finished bonsai commonly displayed in exhibitions. Their unfinished nature is exactly why they belong in this collection.

The first tree carries a future possibility: a root-over-rock composition. That possibility is still a study. No stone has been selected or installed. The living tree will eventually determine what is practical.

This archive will record what happens, not just what looks impressive: dates, mistakes, photographs, seasons, questions, interventions, and the long spans between visible change.

The photographs are coming. Until then, illustrative botanical imagery serves only as creative atmosphere. It will never be confused with a historical record of these trees.

The work of becoming does not require an audience. It requires time.
