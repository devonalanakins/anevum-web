## Decision

On November 18, 2026, the owner selected **The Confluence** from the generated ANEVUM beacon/identity concept board. That is a decision about **direction**: an original field-convergence motif with a central discovery point, calibrated vertical axis and monumental projected-beacon associations. It is **not** approval to publish the exact image-generated linework as a trademark or change the current production logo.

The previous three-tier wedge/Adidas-like aperture family is **REJECTED**. The Open Aperture refined, Axiom and Gate candidates remain in this folder only as historical design work, not active alternatives. Do not accidentally restore their prior "recommended" status.

## Review-ready editable vector suite

- `confluence-full-color.svg`: full institutional signature, with symmetrical field curves, calibrated axis, central node and strong outer frame.
- `confluence-small-color.svg`: purpose-built optical reduction; no fine orbital arch, narrow calibration ticks or tiny strokes that vanish at favicon sizes.
- `confluence-full-mono-ivory.svg`, `confluence-full-mono-ink.svg`, `confluence-small-mono-ivory.svg`, `confluence-small-mono-ink.svg`: genuine one-ink variants for dark surfaces and paper.
- Pure path-based/geometry-based SVG (one circle in the full icon). No external scripts, remote resources, fonts, stock images, trademark assets, or image files embedded.
- Stable `160×160` viewBox, descriptive SVG title/desc. These are manual vector interpretations, **not machine-extracted copies** of the image-generated board. The small mark uses different geometry by design.

## Design constraints / honest gaps

1. Preserve the convergent-field geometry and identifiable center; do **not** return to three mountain/wedge stripes, a sports-app silhouette, generic AI swirl, standard infinity logo, or generic A monogram.
2. Preserve scientific precision while avoiding an over-detailed insignia that becomes a visual insect at 16px. The concept's delicate signal rings may remain in cinematic applications, not the core icon.
3. The image-generated project board is an exploration, not evidence of originality in commerce. Conduct trademark and similarity review before publication.
4. Validate the small icon at 16, 24, 32, 64px in actual browser/app contexts, not only in a large SVG render. Confirm print and one-ink use; human feedback is still pending.
5. Keep **current** `../mark.svg`, source-coupled `../artwork.mjs`, all site and staff identity paths, DNS, public assets and production routes unchanged until the owner explicitly approves the cleaned master and a separate release is verified.
6. No fabricated university accreditation, research credentials, physical research infrastructure, or institutional collaboration.
