02 / The field journal

Slow work.
Visible history.

The meaningful events are often quiet: new growth, a decision not to prune, a changing root system, a season observed.

15 Nov 2026

The first direction: root-over-rock.

For Red Maple No. 01, the design intention is to build an exposed-root composition over a natural stone. Initial work focuses on selecting and studying the rock, not prematurely positioning roots.

Next suitable window

Observe before intervening.

Evaluate root placement at an appropriate future bud-swell repotting window. Record the actual work and observations when it happens.

Always

One photograph at a time.

Each tree gets a consistent dated view so its history can eventually be explored as a visual timeline.

03 / About ANEVUM

A garden of
ongoing work.

By Devon Akins

ANEVUM is a personal bonsai collection and study journal. It begins with five seed-grown red maples and makes room for future trees, techniques, experiments, and seasons.

This is a proposed design. The published site remains unchanged until a reviewed release is approved.
