# ANEVUM Open Institute — Institutional Interface Review

Session: ANEVUM.WEB.DESIGN.2026-11-18.014.INSTITUTIONAL-INTERFACE

## Owner-requested direction (prototype under review)

ANEVUM should present as a serious independent scientific education and research institute, with the navigational precision and editorial confidence of a university website. Do not imitate accreditation, enrollments, faculties, campuses, degrees, employment, equipment ownership or student outcomes that have not been independently verified. Restrict decorative image generation and remove science-fiction facility scenes from the Institute landing and study discovery experience. Favor legible typography, real controls, compact navigation, honest status, carefully labeled research instruments and accessible interaction.

## Implemented in draft PR #816

- Paper-and-ink institutional homepage: academic identity, one real CFP-101 course, three computing lab models (two ideal Boolean experiments and simulated GPU scheduling), one ideal neuroscience model, transparent catalog, direct start/research links and a concise academic method
- Shared white masthead with five actual routes: Institute, Academics, Laboratories, Research, My Studio. Command search remains present. Mobile uses a native disclosure menu.
- Light, restrained editorial palette (ivory, ink, sage, evergreen) on Institute and academic discovery surfaces. Specialized dark instrument benches retain contrast and existing functions.
- The original generated facility design, assets and history are preserved in source and protected preview asset endpoints, but not rendered or requested on the new academic homepage. No WebGL or video is initialized on that page.
- Curriculum course metadata, all three lessons, all laboratory exports, GPU/neuron deterministic validation, local practice notes, the Research Desk and protected owner-only review boundary remain preserved.

## Model versus institution

This is an **independent, non-accredited education and research project**. The current cloud preview requires owner Cloudflare Access sign-in. Intended future educational material is free and open-access, but public release, degree credit, accredited programs, human research recruitment, paid enrollment and scientific/clinical validation are not asserted.

The six fields (computing/systems, AI validation, mathematics/physics, neuroscience, biology/biophysics, neuropsychology) express planned research direction, not six operating academic departments.
